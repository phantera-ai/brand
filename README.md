## In one sentence

**Phantera is B2B prospecting that knows when to stay quiet: five AI agents find, score, write and send, then stop the moment a prospect replies.**

## In one paragraph

Phantera is AI-agent B2B prospecting software made in France. Five agents work in relay: they find the decision makers that match your ideal customer, score every lead from 0 to 100 with a verified email, write and rewrite each message until it is good and you approve it, send one sequence by email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp and SMS from your own accounts, and stop the sequence as soon as a prospect replies. It is built for consulting firms, IT services companies and B2B agencies of 2 to 20 people. The free plan includes 100 leads a month, with no credit card.
